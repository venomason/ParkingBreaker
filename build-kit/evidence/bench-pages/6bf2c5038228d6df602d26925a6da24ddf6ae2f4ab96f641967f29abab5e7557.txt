Option B - Request a Court Hearing: Plead Not Guilty
If you want to plead not guilty and request a court hearing, complete the Application Form and “Request A Court Date” on the back of
this notice and mail it to the address shown on the envelope provided with your ticket . You will receive a letter notify ing you of your
Court hearing date.

Notice: If you appear in C ourt and are found guilty by a preponderance of the evidence, or if you fail to appear , the amount you owe
will increase due to mandatory litigation tax and Court costs that must be assessed, in addition to the fine.
