**Warning: Text Scam Reports**

We have had reports that individuals have been engaging in a caller ID spoofing scam, using Seattle Municipal Court phone numbers, and scams involving text messages, some with QR codes. No one from SMC will call you and ask for your personal information or request money. If you should get one of these calls, please hang up and try calling the number displayed in the caller ID to see where the call is coming from. The FCC website (https://www.fcc.gov/spoofing) is a great resource to learn more about caller ID spoofing, including how to file a complaint if you think you have become a victim of a spoofing scam.

Hearings By Mail

Instead of appearing in person, you have the option of mitigating or contesting your ticket in writing, called adjudication by mail. Once you have scheduled your hearing, you will receive a written statement form with your hearing notice.

Submit the written statement back to the court prior to your hearing date. You may submit the written statement and evidence:

Via the Court's Public Portal, as shown in this video:

By email to CourtCalendar.SMC@seattle.gov.

By mail:

Seattle Municipal Court

ATTN: Magistrate Operations

PO Box 34987

Seattle, WA 98124-4987

By fax: (206) 470-6884

Once submitted, your written statement will be reviewed by a Magistrate. The Magistrate will make a decision and notify you by mail.

You will receive the decision within ten (10) business days from when you submitted your statement. If you do not receive a response, contact the court at (206) 684-5600.

Written statements sent to the court without an initial hearing scheduled will not be read. Instead, a hearing will be set and you will be sent the appropriate written statement form.
