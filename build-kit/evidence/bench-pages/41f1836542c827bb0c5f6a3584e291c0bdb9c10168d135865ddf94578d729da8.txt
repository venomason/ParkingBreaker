The fine must first be paid as a deposit in order for your request to be processed. If the adjudicator rules in your favor, your payment will be refunded. Enclose the full amount due or pay online. Once received, an in-person Hearing will be scheduled with the adjudicator. Hearings are conducted in an informal, non-judicial manner and are normally scheduled for the third Thursday of each month at 5:15 PM. To support your claim, please bring any documentation you believe is relevant including diagrams, maps and photographs. Hearings take place at the Raleigh Parking office located at: 310 W. Martin St Suite 109. A notice will be mailed to you to confirm the date and time of your Hearing.
